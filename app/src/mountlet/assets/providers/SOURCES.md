# Provider icon sources

Provider marks are shown only to identify configured services and open their
web interfaces. Trademarks remain the property of their owners.

- Google Drive: current product icon downloaded from Google's
  <https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/branding> page.
- Dropbox: product mark mirrored by `gilbarbara/logos`; usage follows
  <https://www.dropbox.com/branding>.
- Microsoft OneDrive and Box: product marks mirrored by `gilbarbara/logos`.
- Amazon S3: AWS service icon mirrored by `gilbarbara/logos`, derived from the
  <https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/> set.
- Proton Drive: transparent Proton mark from the Simple Icons distribution,
  based on Proton's <https://proton.me/media/kit> mark.
- Google Photos: current product icon from Google's
  <https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-photos/visual-identity/icon/>.
- iCloud: transparent iCloud mark from the Simple Icons distribution, based on
  Apple's published service mark.
- pCloud: app icon downloaded from <https://www.pcloud.com/>.
- Koofr: app icon downloaded through the branding resources linked from
  <https://koofr.eu/press-information/>.
- ownCloud: app icon downloaded from <https://owncloud.com/>.
- Cloudflare, MinIO, Wasabi, and Nextcloud: product marks from the Simple Icons
  distribution, based on each provider's published mark.

Generic WebDAV and unknown services use Mountlet's generated initial tile.
